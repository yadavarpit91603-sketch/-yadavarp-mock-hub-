Welcome to YadavarpSSB, a platform dedicated to helping defence aspirants prepare for various defence examinations and selection processes.

Our mission is to provide free and accessible preparation resources for candidates aspiring to join the Indian Armed Forces.

🚀 About the Project

YadavarpSSB is an online defence preparation platform designed especially for aspirants preparing for:

- Indian Air Force – Agniveervayu
- Indian Army – Agniveer
- Indian Navy – Agniveer
- NDA Examination
- CDS Examination
- AFCAT Examination
- SSB Interview Preparation

📚 Key Features

- 📝 Phase 1 – Online Mock Tests
- 🎯 Phase 2 – SRT Practice Tests
- ⏱️ Timer-Based Practice
- 📊 Instant Results and Performance Analysis
- 📖 Free Study Materials and PDF Resources
- 🇮🇳 Defence Knowledge and Current Affairs
- 💻 User-Friendly Interface
- 📱 Mobile-Friendly Design

🎯 Our Vision

To make quality defence preparation accessible to every aspirant, regardless of their financial background.

We believe that dedication, discipline, and consistent practice are the keys to success.
